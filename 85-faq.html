How is Oasis Revenue Lab different from just using PriceLabs?

PriceLabs adjusts rates algorithmically, but it cannot tell you whether your strategy is working, whether your floor is correct, or what the market is actually doing. Oasis Revenue Lab sits on top: daily monitoring, market interpretation, floor verification, and clear recommendations. We also configure and manage PriceLabs for you.

Do you change my prices automatically?

Not yet. Today the agent runs in advisory mode — it recommends, you decide. A supervised autopilot is in development in collaboration with PriceLabs, with operator-defined guardrails (your floor, your ceiling, your rules). Existing clients get it first.

What do I need to get started?

An active PriceLabs account and Telegram. We take your listing details and run a short onboarding call to set your floors and goals. Setup takes under 48 hours.

How much does it cost?

AED 80 per listing per month at the founding rate, locked in permanently for the first 20 clients. The standard price rises after launch. Hybrid and consultancy engagements are priced custom to your portfolio.

Do you only cover Dubai?

Currently, yes. Our deepest data covers JBR, Dubai Marina, Downtown, Business Bay, and Palm Jumeirah. GCC expansion is planned for 2026.

I already have a property manager. Does this still work?

Yes — Oasis Revenue Lab works alongside your property manager as the revenue intelligence layer. If your PM handles pricing, we can work with them directly on the strategy layer.

How long until I see results?

Briefings start within 48 hours. Portfolios that are underpriced or missing floor discipline typically improve within 2–4 weeks. Outcomes are tracked via Hostaway.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Service is monthly rolling — cancel with 30 days notice. The founding rate is locked for 18 months for launch-phase clients, and after that it stays at your signed rate.

Do you work with properties outside holiday homes?

Our focus is short-term rentals on Airbnb and Booking.com priced with PriceLabs. Serviced apartments and boutique hotels are on the roadmap.

Who is behind Oasis Revenue Lab?

Oasis Revenue Lab is a division of Lux Oasis Advisory & Services LLC, a UAE-registered company. We operate Lux Oasis Holiday Homes, a DTCM-licensed operation — and every system runs on our own portfolio first.
